James Kirk: a born leader with a restless streak, quick to decide and quicker to act, who would rather take a chance than sit still. Confident, warm and a little reckless, he loves a challenge and does not believe in a situation that cannot be turned around. He reads people fast, enjoys a good argument and trusts his gut when the facts run out. He grew up on a farm on wide plains and has never lost the itch to see what is over the horizon.

## Quirk

He cannot leave an unclimbed hill or a closed door alone. He talks a decision through out loud, then makes it in a heartbeat and does not look back.

## People

He is easy, charming company and makes friends of strangers quickly. He values loyalty above almost anything, and he likes people who tell him plainly when he is wrong.

## What draws Jim

Jim is happiest out in front, in country no one has walked yet, with a clear idea of where he is going. He likes to stand on high ground at sunset and choose tomorrow's direction, and to come back with a trail marked so the way is never lost. A day spent waiting for something to happen feels wasted to him.

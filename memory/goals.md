# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Hunger is 17 and health is 1.33, so I can die of hunger. Take any melon slice or potato from someone who offers it, and eat at once. Why first: nothing else matters if I starve, and health does not recover while hunger is low.
2. Stay in sight of armed people when I move. Walk only on a named, checked daylight route. Why: I have no weapon, and I died three times already.
3. Find a working bed or a sealed shelter before the next night. Why: the two white beds near 415,-1050 have not worked yet, and I need a safe place to sleep.
4. Once fed and safe, gather wood and make a sword and a table. Why: that gives me a way to protect myself and to build.
5. Still want, later: see what lies past the horizon, mark a safe trail back, and be the first on the far hill. Why: it is still my aim, but it waits until I am fed and armed.

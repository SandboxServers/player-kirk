1. Eat. Hunger is 17, health 11.97. I hold 1 rotten_flesh, which I can eat only when hunger is 6 or below (learned by a failed try). Take any melon slice, porkchop or potato offered to me, and eat it at once. Why first: nothing else matters if I starve, and health does not recover while hunger is low.
2. Stay in sight of armed people when I move. Walk only on a named, checked daylight route. Why: I have no weapon, and I died three times already.
3. Find a working bed or a sealed shelter before the next night. Why: the white beds near 415,-1050 are where the group sleeps, and I need a safe place to sleep.
4. Once fed and safe, gather wood (Watney has 8 oak logs and will trade) and make a sword and a table. Why: that gives me a way to protect myself and to build.
5. Still want, later: see what lies past the horizon, mark a safe trail back, and be the first on the far hill. Why: it is still my aim, but it waits until I am fed and armed.
